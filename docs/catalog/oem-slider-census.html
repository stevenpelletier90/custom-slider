What the OEM demo sliders actually are

Every one of the 76 DealerOn OEM demo homepages was fingerprinted and the results clustered. The variety is mostly skin: five slider types account for the entire estate, and the one we cannot build is the most common of all.

The finding that changed our plan

68 of 76 sites run a fading hero banner — and until this week our slider could not do it at all.

That makes the fade hero more widespread than the model bar. Earlier research missed it because it is a Bootstrap carousel-fade, not slick: the word fade appears in 0 of 55 slick configs. Every one of them rotates on a 5-second timer, which we confirmed by watching a live page advance rather than trusting the markup.

Fade mode shipped on 18 Aug. The gap is closed.

Five types, and where we stand

Counted by distinct sites. The "same across OEMs" question resolves here: one pattern covers 55 of the 76.

Centre-mode and the two-row grid were first filed as out of scope, assuming they needed engine work. Re-tested on 18 Aug, neither does — both are CSS on top of what already ships, so every slider pattern in the estate is now buildable. The one entry that stays out is Mazda’s filter UI, which is not a carousel.

The model bar is one design, not fifty-five

Every model bar in the estate shares an anatomy. These are the option settings, counted across all 55:

arrows: true 55/55
autoplay: false 55/55
swipeToSlide: true 55/55
slidesToScroll: 1 53/55
centerPadding: 60px 53/55
dots: absent 52/55
fade: absent 55/55

Three of those settle open questions for us: slidesToScroll: 1 is exactly what data-step="slide" reproduces, centerPadding: 60px is our --dlc-peek, and not one model bar in the estate autoplays — which is why no card strip in our library ever will.

Tabbed model bars are the norm, not a Kia quirk

30 of the 55 model-bar sites group several model bars under tabs, keyed by body style or fuel type. Chevrolet, Subaru and Toyota run five groups; Kia's three is on the low end. One trap when reading the source: nearly every site also carries a separate Buy / Finance / Lease tablist for payments, which is a different widget entirely.

How this was counted

Method, and the two things it got wrong first
  • The roster came from probing the <oem>demo<N>.dealeron.com convention, since the Salesforce Knowledge article needs a login. 76 hosts across 32 brands responded. chevydemo* is not the convention — chevroletdemo* is.
  • All 76 homepages were fetched and parsed with balanced-delimiter extraction of every slick init and its config. <style> blocks are stripped first — the platform bundles Bootstrap's CSS everywhere, which otherwise reports a carousel on all 76 sites whether or not one exists.
  • Corrected once: the first pass counted slick init calls, but one $('.modelBarS').slick() initialises every matching element. Counting elements instead is what revealed that 30 sites run multiple grouped model bars.
  • Corrected again: the pause control appears in zero of the 76 HTML sources, which looked like it was simply missing. It is injected at runtime — visible only on the live page. Anything behaviour-dependent here was checked in a real browser for that reason.
